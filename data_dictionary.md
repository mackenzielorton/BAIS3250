| Column  | Type    | Source       | Description                                                                                 |
|---------|---------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Date    | Date    | Kaggle       | Trending data for each observation of Tesla’s stock price.                                  |
| Close   | Numeric | Kaggle       | Closing price of Tesla stock at the end of the trading day (USD).                           |

| Low     | Numeric | Kaggle       | Lowest price reached by Tesla stock during the trading session (USD).                       |

| Return  | Numeric | Kaggle       | Daily return calculated as the percentage change in closing price from the previous day.    |
| EPS     | Numeric | Macrotrends  | Tesla’s earnings per share for the quarter (USD).                                           |
| Revenue | Numeric | Macrotrends  | Tesla’s total revenue for the quarter, in millions of U.S. dollars.                         |
